Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

In short: Tessera does not collect, store or send any of your data to us. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything Tessera keeps stays on your devices.

BitTorrent itself is a public, peer-to-peer network. When you download or share a torrent, other people in that torrent can see your IP address. What other people can see explains what that means.

This policy covers the Tessera apps for Mac, iPhone and iPad, including the Share extension and widgets. "We" and "us" mean the developer of Tessera.

What we collect

Nothing. Tessera has no servers of its own, no sign-in and no analytics or advertising software. We never receive your torrents, your files, your downloads or any information about how you use the app.

What Tessera stores on your device

  • Your torrents and their progress. Tessera saves the list of torrents you add, which pieces are complete, your priorities and similar details, so it can continue after you quit or restart.
  • Downloaded files. Files are saved to the folder you choose. On Mac the default is the Tessera folder in your home folder. On iPhone and iPad they are in the app's own folder, visible in the Files app under On My iPhone or On My iPad.
  • Settings, such as speed limits and download folders, in the app's preferences.
  • Paired devices. If you use remote control, the keys that let your iPhone and Mac recognise each other are stored in the Keychain on each device.
  • Items you share to Tessera. On iPhone and iPad, a torrent file or magnet link you send through the Share Sheet is kept in a folder shared between the Share extension and the app, until the app adds it.

Removing a torrent deletes Tessera's saved information about it. You can choose whether to delete the downloaded files too. Deleting the app removes everything it stored, except files saved in folders outside the app, such as the Tessera folder on Mac.

What other people can see when you use BitTorrent

To download and share, Tessera connects directly to other computers ("peers") and to services that help find them. This is how BitTorrent works in every app, and it means:

  • Your IP address and port are visible to peers you connect to, to trackers listed in the torrent, and to the distributed hash table (DHT) network.
  • The torrents you download or share can be seen by those peers, trackers and the DHT, because they are identified by their info-hash.
  • The name and version of the app (for example "Tessera/1.0" and the libtorrent version) is shared with peers and trackers, as all BitTorrent clients do.

Tessera is not an anonymity tool. If you need to hide your IP address, use a VPN or another network service you trust. We do not operate trackers or peers, and we cannot see or control what they record.

Features that use your network

  • DHT and peer exchange find more peers for your torrents. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • Router port forwarding (UPnP and NAT-PMP) asks your router to let peers connect to you. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Local peer discovery (Mac only) looks for peers on your local network. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Remote control lets your iPhone or iPad control downloads on your Mac. The devices find each other on your local network with Bonjour. You pair them by matching a code shown on both screens, and everything they send each other after that is encrypted. The connection goes directly between your devices, never through us.
  • Share extension. If you share a web link to a .torrent file, the extension downloads that file from the address you shared.

Notifications and Live Activities

Download notifications on Mac, and Live Activities and the Dynamic Island on iPhone, are created on your device. They are not sent through us.

Children

Tessera is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will ship with the app and the date at the top will change. Because we collect no data, a change can't affect data we already hold about you.
